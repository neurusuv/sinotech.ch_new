<h1 style="text-align:center">第<span>12</span>届中国留学人员广州科技交流会欢迎您！ </h1>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">第十二届留交会举办时间、地点、规模</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">举办时间：<span>2009</span>年<span>12</span>月<span>26-28</span>日</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">举办地点：中国进出口商品交易会流花展馆</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">邀请海外高层次留学人员：<span>1200-1500</span>人</span></p>
<p><strong><u><span style="font-size:12pt;color:red;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">获邀人士将获得大会提供的会期免费食宿服务及大会统一安排的航班接送礼遇；</span></u></strong><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"> </span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">第十二届留交会主要内容</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>1.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">海外留学高层次人才招聘；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>2.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">留学人员与国内单位合作项目洽谈；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>3.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">各地创业投资环境展示和推介；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>4.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">国家“千人计划”及相关政策信息发布；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>5.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">第四届“春晖杯”留学人员创新创业大赛项目对接洽谈暨颁奖活动；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>6.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">各地科技难题项目招贤；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>7.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">留学人员回国创业成就展；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>8.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">高端国际学术论坛、报告会；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>9.<span style="font:normal normal normal 7pt/normal 'Times New Roman'">      </span></span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">主协办单位专场活动。</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">留学人员报名条件：</span></strong><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"><span>    </span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">已取得海外硕士或以上学位，在国外大学、大企业或研究机构从事专业工作三年以上的海内外中国留学人员及海外华人专才。重点邀请带自主知识产权项目、有学术成就的留学人员（也包括能代表海外大企业寻找国内合作伙伴者及新生代华人专才）参会。</span><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"> </span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">如何报名</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:Verdana">①</span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%"><font face="Times New Roman">    </font></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">网上报名：</span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">A.</span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（新会员）登录大会网站（<span>http:// <span>www.ocs-gz.gov.cn</span></span>或<span><a href="http://www.liujiaohui.com/">http://www.liujiaohui.com</a></span>）注册成为“个人”用户<span>;</span></span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">B.</span><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">（旧用户）：直接登陆系统，更新您的个人资料即可。</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">② 其他报名方式：通过留交会网站下载或其他渠道获得留学人员参会报名表，一表一人，一表多人无效，填写后发回组委会报名点，同时附最高学历证书复印件等资料作为申请辅助材料。</span><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"> </span></strong></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">留交会服务</span></strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">1)</span><span style="font-size:12pt"><font face="Times New Roman">   </font></span><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">获邀人士将获得大会提供的会期免费食宿服务及大会统一安排的航班接送礼遇；未获邀人士欢迎向组委会免费索取专业人士入场券，参与大会论坛、展览等活动，并自行承担食宿费用。</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">2)</span><span style="font-size:12pt"><font face="Times New Roman">    </font></span><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">参会留学人员都可在留交会会刊及<span>OCS</span>网站免费发布求职、项目合作信息；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">3)</span><span style="font-size:12pt"><font face="Times New Roman">    </font></span><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">获邀留学人员免费参与留交会项目推介会，有机会获得“<span>10</span>分钟宣传推介”黄金机会；</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">4)</span><span style="font-size:12pt"><font face="Times New Roman">    </font></span><span style="font-size:12pt;font-family:仿宋_GB2312">留学人员随行家属可以大会优惠价享受留学人员同等服务。</span><strong><span style="font-size:12pt;color:#003399;line-height:150%;font-family:仿宋_GB2312"> </span></strong></p>
