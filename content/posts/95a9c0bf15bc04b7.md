<div>Dear all CALSS members and Friends,</div>

For more information of Prof Ruan please look at  <a href="http://www.gis.a-star.edu.sg/internet/site/investigators.php?user_id=19&amp;f=cv"> http://www.gis.a-star.edu.sg/internet/site/investigators.php?user_id=19&amp;f=cv</a>
