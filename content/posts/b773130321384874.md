<p>As announced already, the upcoming meeting for the 7<sup>th</sup> CASTS General Assembly(瑞士中国学人科技协会2011年年会) will be on Saturday, 17 Sep. 2011 between 10am and 16pm. The updated Agenda is enclosed here. If you haven’t done it, please make your online registration at: <a href="https://spreadsheets.google.com/spreadsheet/viewform?formkey=dFV5QUFpVlFNMk9lOFBaM0xTbDN1TUE6MA">( clike HERE ) </a> before Saturday, 10 Setember 2011.</p>
<p>All CASTS members are warmly invited to participate in this important annual conference. As usual, student members ofCASTS will get the standard CASTS travel allowance.</p>
<p>All CASTS members who are government-sponsored (公派留学人员) will also get the travel allowance for attending the annual meeting.</p>
