<p>在中瑞建交65 周年之际，瑞士中国学人科技协会定于10 月24 日在伯尔尼举办科协十一届年会，本次年会的主题是”中瑞关系中的未来机会和挑战”暨庆祝中瑞建交65周年。我们诚挚邀请并殷切盼望您能在百忙之中拨冗出席我们的年会。应邀出席本次科协年会的嘉宾有: 中瑞自由贸易协定谈判代表,瑞士对外经济贸易部特别对外经济署的Christian Etter 大使先生,瑞士行业协会组织秘书长Hans-Ulrich Bigler 先生, 苏黎世联邦理工大学化学与应用生物科学Hansjörg Grützmacher 教授, 新华都商学院理事长何志毅教授等。(详细见所附日程单)。</p>
<p>欢迎大家积极通过下列按钮报名参会,报名截止日期为10月16日(周五)。</p>

<p>On the joyful occasion of the 65th anniversary of China-Switzerland bilateral relations, Chinese Association of Science and Technology in Switzerland (CASTS) would like to cordially invite you as the distinguished guest attedning our 11th annual meeting on October 24, 2015 in Bern. The topics of this year’s Annual Meeting is “Future opportunities in Sino-Swiss relationship”.</p>
<p>The detailed program is attached for your consideration.A music performance will be organized as well.</p>
<p>Additional information about the meeting, together with details about how to get to the venue at the University of Bern are enclosed. I would be extremely grateful if you could R.S.V.P by no later than Friday 16 October to</p>
<p>Look forward to seeing you!</p>
