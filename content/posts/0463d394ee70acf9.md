<p><span style="font-size:medium"><span lang="ZH-CN">瑞士中国学人材料科技协会（简称材料分会）是瑞士中国学人科技协会的分支组织机构之一。协会旨在发挥在瑞士材料科学领域的华人学生学者的专业优势，促进中瑞材料科学领域的交流与合作；开展各项材料科学领域的科技活动</span><span lang="EN-US">,</span><span lang="ZH-CN">促进会员之间的交流与沟通。本次年会将对协会自</span><span lang="EN-US">2013</span><span lang="ZH-CN">年成立以来所做的工作和取得的成就进行总结。协会将通过本次年会更名为</span><span lang="EN-US">“</span><span lang="ZH-CN">材料与能源科技协会</span><span lang="EN-US">”,</span><span lang="ZH-CN">以充分发挥协会的平台作用，进一步促进材料和能源等其他领域的跨学科交流。</span><span lang="ZH-CN">会议还邀请以下几位活跃在瑞士</span>/<span lang="ZH-CN">中国材料与能源领域的中外科学家带来精彩的学术报告。</span></span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US"><span style="color:#000000;font-family:Calibri;font-size:medium">The Chinese Association of Materials Science and Technology in Switzerland (CAMSTS) was founded in spring 2013. It is the material branch of the CASTS (Chinese Association of Science and Technology, Switzerland). Our aim is to promote communication and collaboration of science and technology between Switzerland and China. Recently, we are planning to expand our focus to include the field of energy science and, therefore, the name of the association will be updated correspondingly. The new name and the new organization will be announced during the meeting. Following well-known scientists in the fields of material and energy are also invited to give keynote speeches</span></span></p>
